Media rules

How Dodo TV handles uploaded files.

  • Only relative paths below the upload folder are stored in the database.
  • Posters are served from /media/… and videos from Range-enabled /stream/… routes.
  • Uploaded videos are validated with FFprobe. Invalid files are rejected, not silently kept.
  • Deleting a movie, show, season or episode also removes its uploaded files.
  • Files under temp/ are always treated as orphaned.
